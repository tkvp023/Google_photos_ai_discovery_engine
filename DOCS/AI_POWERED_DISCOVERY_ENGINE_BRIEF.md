# AI-Powered Discovery Engine — Project Brief

## Overview
Before proposing any product solution or feature wireframes, build an **AI-powered system** that analyzes user feedback and conversations about photo retrieval at scale.

## Permitted AI Stack
You may use:
* Claude
* GPTs
* Autonomous Agents
* Orchestrated Workflows
* RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
* n8n
* Zapier
* Perplexity
* Any AI-native stack of your choice

## Public Data Sources to Ingest & Analyze
* Google Play Store reviews (`com.google.android.apps.photos`)
* Apple App Store reviews (`id962194608`)
* Reddit discussions (`r/googlephotos`, `r/GooglePixel`, `r/androidapps`, `r/techsupport`, etc.)
* Google Photos community / support forum discussions
* Social media conversations
* YouTube comments and video discussions
* Forums and other relevant public communities

## Core Questions Your Discovery Engine Must Uncover:
1. **What kinds of old photos do users struggle to retrieve?**
2. **What information do people actually remember about a photo?**
3. **What information have they forgotten?**
4. **How do users formulate searches when their memory is incomplete?**

## Key Evaluation Standard
> **Beyond Surface Summaries:** Your workflow must go beyond generic review summarization or shallow sentiment analysis. It should enable you to identify, cluster, and compare different retrieval problems and opportunity areas using quantified evidence and verbatim quotes from real users.
